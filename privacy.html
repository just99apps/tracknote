Privacy Policy

TrackNote

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TrackNote ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

TrackNote is designed to help users create, organize, and manage notes. Depending on the features you use, the app may store the following information on your device:

  • Notes and written content
  • Audio recordings and voice notes
  • Images and photos attached to notes
  • Reminder and notification settings
  • User preferences and app settings
  • Other content you choose to create or import into the app

Your notes and associated content are stored locally on your device where supported by the app. If optional online services, cloud synchronization, or backup features are provided and enabled, relevant information may be processed or stored by those services.

We do not claim that all information remains exclusively on your device if you use features that transmit information to external services.

2. Permissions Used by the App

TrackNote requests permissions to provide specific app features. Permissions are used for their intended purposes. Access to sensitive features is subject to your device settings and applicable Android permission requirements.

Microphone / Audio Recording

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO

This permission allows TrackNote to access the microphone when you use audio recording or voice note features.

  • Audio is recorded when you initiate a recording feature.
  • Recordings may be saved locally for playback and reference.
  • Microphone access is not required for ordinary text notes.
  • Audio may be processed by an external service if you use a feature that explicitly requires such processing.

Camera

  • android.permission.CAMERA

This permission allows you to take photos or capture images for attachment to your notes, where supported.

The camera is accessed when you use a feature that requires it. TrackNote does not need camera access for ordinary text-based note creation.

Access to Images and Media

  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

These permissions support selecting or importing images from your device for use within the app, subject to your Android version and the access permissions granted.

TrackNote accesses images when needed for features such as attaching photos to notes or importing image files. Where supported, users may choose specific images rather than grant access to an entire media library.

Biometric Authentication

  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC

This permission supports biometric authentication, such as fingerprint or other supported biometric verification, if the app provides an app-lock or authentication feature.

Biometric verification is handled through Android's authentication system. TrackNote does not receive or store your fingerprint data or other underlying biometric information.

Exact Alarm Permissions

  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
  • android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM

These permissions support scheduling reminders that need to occur at specific times, where permitted by Android.

They may be used for note reminders, scheduled notifications, and other time-sensitive features provided by TrackNote.

Notification Permission

  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

This permission allows TrackNote to display notifications, such as:

  • Scheduled note reminders
  • Task or event reminders, if supported
  • Other notifications related to app functionality

You can manage notification permissions and notification settings through your device settings.

Device Restart Detection

  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

This permission allows TrackNote to receive a notification from Android when the device has finished restarting.

If supported by the app, this may be used to restore or reschedule previously configured reminders and alarms after a device restart. It does not grant access to your personal messages or contacts.

Storage and File Access

  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Where supported by your Android version, these permissions may be used for features such as:

  • Exporting notes
  • Saving files or attachments
  • Importing notes or supported files
  • Creating local backups

Android storage permissions and restrictions vary by operating system version. TrackNote uses the available platform mechanisms to access files needed for supported features.

Google Play Billing

  • com.android.vending.BILLING

This permission supports Google Play Billing if TrackNote offers paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Purchases are handled through Google Play. Transaction and purchase information may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies and applicable payment terms.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by TrackNote may be used to provide the app's features, including:

  • Creating, editing, and organizing notes
  • Recording and playing voice notes
  • Attaching images to notes
  • Setting and displaying reminders
  • Protecting access to the app through biometric authentication
  • Importing, exporting, or backing up content
  • Managing app preferences and settings
  • Providing optional paid features, if available

4. Data Storage and Retention

Notes, recordings, images, and other content may be stored locally on your device. Their retention depends on your use of the app, available storage, and your device's settings.

Deleting a note or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored content. However, exported files, device backups, or information stored by optional third-party services may remain separately.

If cloud storage or synchronization is offered, the applicable service's retention and deletion practices may also apply.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

TrackNote does not sell your personal information.

Depending on the features implemented and used, the app may rely on third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchases
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics services
  • Analytics services, if enabled
  • Cloud backup or synchronization providers, if offered
  • Audio transcription or other processing services, if offered

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

TrackNote does not intentionally share note content, audio recordings, or images with third parties unless required to provide a feature you choose, you authorize the sharing, or disclosure is required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Depending on the features available, protection may include local storage controls, Android device security, and biometric authentication.

You should protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep your operating system and app updated.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
